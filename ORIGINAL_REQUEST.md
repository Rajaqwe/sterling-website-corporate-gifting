# Sterling B2B Corporate Gifting Platform — Specification & Requirements

## Overview
Sterling is a luxury B2B corporate gifting, employee appreciation, and promotional merchandise platform built to streamline corporate gift curation, volume tiered quotations, sample ordering, digital artwork proofing, and enterprise checkout.

## Core Pillars
1. **Catalog & Discovery**:
   - High-end corporate gifting product catalog with categories, MOQ filtering, dynamic search, and tiered pricing visibility.
   - Product detail views with image gallery, detailed specifications, branding options, and volume pricing tables.

2. **Customization & Artwork Workflow**:
   - Custom branding options (Laser engraving, Screen printing, Debossing, Foil stamping, Embroidery).
   - Artwork file upload and digital mockups.

3. **B2B Quotation Engine**:
   - Instant quote calculations factoring volume tier discounts, setup fees, and customization costs.
   - Formal PDF quote generation and automated account manager routing.

4. **Cart & Enterprise Checkout**:
   - B2B cart handling MOQ validation, tiered volume price resolution, and multi-address drop-shipping support.
   - Secure checkout with standard invoicing, purchase order (PO), and direct payment workflows.

5. **Role-Based Admin Management**:
   - Super Admin, Admin, and Operations access controls.
   - Product catalog management, order processing, quote approval, customer management, and CSV data exports.
